FAQ

Good to know

Still curious? Here's what people usually ask before signing up.

Is MotoTrack really free?

Yes — creating an account, adding vehicles, tracking services and getting email alerts are all free, with no card required.

Which vehicles are supported?

Cars, bikes/motorcycles and tractors, across petrol, diesel, CNG, electric and hybrid fuel types — each with its own fuel-aware service catalogue.

How do reminders reach me?

By email — a warning before a service is due, an urgent reminder as it closes in, and a critical alert if it becomes overdue.

Can I change a service interval?

Yes. Every built-in interval can be overridden per vehicle, and you can add entirely custom services unique to your ride.

What if my odometer reading is out of date?

MotoTrack calculates due dates from your last saved reading — so do keep it updated. We'll gently remind you to, too.
